# The panel and its windows

The always-on-top panel, drawing, settings window, captions mode, History, keys, clipboard and icons. Code and tests outrank this page.

| Path | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/mod.rs` | The always-on-top panel window: clicks, drag-selecting text, scrolling, and the captions key, Ctrl+Win by default (hold to dictate (nothing is typed while the captions key is held: dictated lines are planned and the step queue runs once its modifiers are all up, `KeyEvent::LetGo`, since a held key's auto-repeat slips into the moment `press` lets it go (a held Win opened Search); Ctrl+Win+Z drops what still waits instead of erasing; the planned steps run in order, a paste then a key press, 150 ms apart when there are several; undo takes back only a paste); +V paste what you said, again to reach further back; Z undo; S stop/start; C lock; H hide; Q quit; B save a miss; T switch to captions mode and back), rebindable in settings. Each app's header folds it (chevron) and, double-clicked, shows it alone; its other buttons show in a toolbar while it is hovered, among them block (⊘), which blocks the app (stops capturing and captioning it, history kept) after asking "Just this session / Always / Cancel" with "Don't ask again". The footer's keyboard button opens the Shortcuts tab. Buttons show pushed in while the mouse is down on them, and a tooltip once the pointer rests on one for 400 ms (one timer for both windows). Redraw requests post one WM_DRAW, so a burst of them draws once (the panel and, if open, the settings window), and timers ask for a frame only when what they show changed (a Live badge going out, a quiet app folding, a tooltip due). The panel never takes the keyboard (WS_EX_NOACTIVATE) except while one of History's boxes is being typed in: clicking a box lets it activate, and Escape, leaving History or another window activating gives the keyboard back. While the settings window's font list is open, keys go to its search (the same `field.rs` editing as History's boxes); Enter picks the first match, and Escape clears the search, then closes the list. A theme colour picked edits the Custom theme (made from the preset shown if need be). Quitting during a download asks to pause it until next time; logging off just keeps the queue. Notices the decoder posts (a GPU fallback) show in the status; a newer version the update check found is offered in a card over the panel (Update / Later). The panel hands the hook the macros' shortcuts after the macros are read (the decoder reads them each poll and tells it; a shortcut Kiku refuses is told once by name and reason), runs a `KeyEvent::Macro` with `plan_macro` on the step queue, and plans the mic lines heard without the dictation key with `heard_steps` (the decoder posts WM_TYPE for them too). The macro palette (`palette.rs`: `Palette::open` over `Macros::available`, `steps` by `plan_macro`; `View::palette`) opens with Ctrl+Win+M (again closes it) or the "what can I say" phrase (`Executor::show_palette` sets a flag the panel acts on), for the app and mode in front, drawn by `render/macros.rs` as a popover with a row per macro (`Action::RunFromPalette`: runs it on the step queue); any other click closes it, and Escape doesn't (the panel takes no keys). `Panel::mode` shows as a chip in the title bar unless Normal. One dispatch: `on_captions_key`, each `Outcome::actions` of a step and the timer at `Dispatch::next_wake` (`DISPATCH_TIMER`, replacing the dictate timer) all go through `Dispatch`, then `perform_kiku` does what comes back (command keys use `Command::action`); a Dictating badge shows while dictation is latched, with how to stop it (`stop_hint`) |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/render.rs` | Direct2D/DirectWrite drawing of the panel and the settings window on the CPU (software DC render target): every frame is laid out, but only the bands that differ from the last frame are drawn and pushed to the layered window with UpdateLayeredWindowIndirect; text layouts are cached between frames. One `Renderer` holds the shared resources and the frame's palette; each window keeps a `Surface` (its frame, last scene and layouts), drawn whole again when the theme changes. The palette is worked out only when the themes change (Custom's is cached, also for its tile); the panel draws with it see-through as its opacities say (the locked pair while locked or while one of its sliders is dragged; `Palette::see_through`: the window's background and the text, each on its own; scrolled text is cut, not faded, over a see-through background). Buttons are line icons (`render/icon.rs`): icon buttons rest bare and lift a plate under the pointer, text buttons are primary (accent glass), secondary (plate) or ghost; the title bar holds the app's buttons, then the window's, and the "N hidden" chip; a hovered section header or line shows its buttons (copy, correct, Ask Claude, pin, hide, block) in a floating toolbar, and a selection Correct and Ask Claude; your lines in an app's section are marked as `your_lines` says (italics, and/or a band of the accent with a bar at its left, `Palette::yours`); a line's name ("You:", "Speaker 2:", "Mic 2:": `Caption::label_len` says how much of the text it is, and `display_len` sizes the row estimate) is drawn in its own brush, the accent for "You" and the speaker's colour (`Palette::speakers`, by voice) for the rest; Live is an accent badge; tooltips are popovers under the button; settings tabs are a nav bar of icons, on/off settings are switches, the rest segmented; shortcuts are drawn as key caps; the block prompt is a card over a shade with primary, secondary and ghost buttons; a speaker's name is drawn as the label style says (`labels.rs`; clicking it, or the badge or rail, opens a text box over the line: Enter names the voice through `Controls::name_voice`, Escape cancels; with the model on another computer a notice says names need it on this PC, and the first voice at your mic is "You" and can't be named), scroll areas that overflow show a thin thumb, and a section scrolled up from its newest line a Latest button that takes it back down to follow it again. Collapsed, the title bar shows the newest line |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/render/design.rs` | The look's one source of truth: a `Palette` per app theme (three text levels, one accent and the colour on it, warning and error, the `Glass` surfaces: window, title band, card, the settings nav bar, raised, popover, button plate, pressed, primary and danger, recessed track, chosen segment, knob; overlays and hairlines, shade and shadow in white and black on dark themes, in the theme's ink on light ones, which also get a window edge), worked out by `dark`/`light` from a few hex values each (the background and accent are `theme.rs`'s). Custom's palette is its base preset's with its colours: a background of its own brings surfaces, text and shading worked out from it (dark or light by its lightness), an accent of its own the colour on it and the accent-tinted surfaces; `speakers`: six colours for the names of a section's speakers (amber, violet, coral, green, pink, blue; bright on dark themes, deep on light ones, `speaker_colours(on_dark)`), faded with the text; radii, the 4-DIP spacing grid and type sizes. The window is opaque so scrolled captions can fade into it |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/render/icon.rs` | Every button's icon: Lucide line icons (ISC) as path data, built once into Direct2D geometry and stroked 1.5 DIPs wide with round caps at any size; ✨ "Ask Claude" is `Sparkles`, drawn in the accent colour |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/render/subtitles.rs` | Captions mode's drawing: a glossy pill per line sized to its text (rim, gradient and highlight worked out from one colour), newest at the bottom, the oldest shown cut to its last rows; app icons, a line's name ("You:" as before; "Speaker N:" and "Mic N:" bold in the speaker's colour, picked for the pill's lightness and drawn on a layout of its own so the halo stays plain); a soft text halo that strengthens as the pills get see-through; the drag tab (glossy like the pills, with a grip); the Appearance tab's theme tiles (each theme's window, card, accent and captions pill in miniature; a "+" tile makes Custom until there is one) and the theme's four colour swatches (each opens a palette of suggestions, opening upwards when there is no room below, with Windows' picker for any other); captions mode's rows in settings under a preview pill (an opacity slider); the font list (presets, then installed families, listed once; typing searches it, and it opens on the chosen font) |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/subtitles.rs` | Captions mode's windows: the lines (always click-through, two-thirds of their screen wide, bottom-centre by default, saved where dragged) and the grip tab beside them that drags them (double-clicked, it brings the panel back). Shown while there is something to read; with auto-hide on (the default), 4 s after the last change they fade by re-blending the drawn frame at lower opacity (no redraw), and the next speech starts afresh. The panel hides while it is on; the panel window owns its timers and gets its windows' messages |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/settings.rs` | The settings window the gear opens: movable, owned by the panel (so in front of it), with a nav bar of tabs General (mode: captions and dictation, or dictation only; the Dictation card; save mode, updates: the daily check, the version and Check now, Run setup again), Listening (sensitivity meters; the models, each with Download and its progress, Pause, Cancel, Retry or a two-press Delete, the device picker and Add a model; the GPU card with the card and driver found, Run models on GPU or CPU, and the GPU pack's download; Where models run, This PC or Another computer with the host's code box and what's happening, and Share this PC's models with its code, Copy and a two-press New code, drawn by `render/remote.rs`, which never draws a code's secret), Apps (Speakers: switches for telling speakers apart in apps and at your mic, saved at once and, when on with the speaker model missing, fetching it, with its Download, progress and Pause shown while it isn't here; auto-fold time, what the block button does, the blocked apps with Unblock, how your lines are marked and the pinned apps with Unpin), Words (Listen for a word: a box and Add hotword, Enter too, with a strength slider (the `@` words' range, the settings' strength unless moved), the one way to add a hotword by itself; Fix a mistake: what was heard and what it should be, filled in by a line's or the selection's Correct, then Add a rule, everywhere or only where the line came from, and Also listen for the fix, each added on its own and explained; where word rules and transforms apply and the `@` words' strength (a slider), a Try box showing what a line becomes in captions and dictation, everywhere and per app (dictation's rows come from `Macros::preview`, so a macro's phrase shows as `[its name]`), Open file, Copy instructions for an assistant, and the file's entries by section (actions among them, as a command or a snippet, shown by phrase and description, never a snippet's text), each removable, each `@` word with a slider for a strength of its own (written to the file when let go) and a reset back to the settings'; drawn by `render/words.rs`), Macros (see `macro_tab.rs`), Appearance (the app's theme, the panel's window and text opacity, unlocked and locked, captions mode) and Shortcuts. On first run (no settings saved) and from General it walks through setup instead: Keys, Model (the recommendation for this PC, picked), GPU and Saving (with updates), each with its default, Skip setup, Back and Next; leaving the model step downloads the model picked. Drawn by the panel's renderer on its own surface; the panel's window state handles its messages |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/view.rs` | A window's UI state (the panel's, or the settings window's): screen or tab, scroll positions, the button hovered (and whether its tooltip is due) or pressed, clickable regions (and where an area opened at a row was scrolled to, which the view keeps), and the font list's search (its `Field` and the fonts matching it, worked out only when it changes) |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/finder.rs` | History's finding: the search box (full-text, the word being typed matching longer words) with who (everyone, you, an app, an app with your replies) and when (today, 7 days, all) filters, results paged newest first, SQL mode with example queries, copying or sharing what was found, deleting a session. Every query runs on a thread of its own over a read-only connection (deleting opens one that writes); a newer search replaces older ones waiting, and pages of a search typed over are dropped |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/field.rs` | A text box's text and caret, edited by keys (characters, word-wise Backspace, Delete and arrows, Home, End, paste): History's search and SQL boxes, the font list's search and the Words tab's Try box |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/render/history.rs` | History's drawing: the Search / SQL switch, the search box (caret, focus ring, clear), filters with the count and copy and ✨ for all, past sessions (apps, start, length, lines; delete asks once more) or results (text from shortly before the first match, matches marked; copy and ✨ on hover; a click opens the session at that line, marked), SQL's box, examples, Run and its rows as a table whose columns share the width. `draw_text_box` draws any text box from its `BoxRole`, the font list's search too |
| `assets/icon/`, `scripts/make-icon.py`, `crates/app/src/overlay/app_icon.rs` | Kiku's icon. `kiku.svg` is the source, a glossy jelly speech bubble, with simplified cuts `kiku-24.svg` (20 and 24 px) and `kiku-16.svg`; `uv run scripts/make-icon.py` renders them into `kiku.ico` (16 to 256 px) and preview PNGs, which `crates/app/build.rs` embeds in `kiku.exe`. `app_icon(size)` loads it: window classes (through `register_class`), the tray and the title bars' mark use it, and so must anything new that shows Kiku's icon |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/icons.rs`, `tray.rs` | App icons from executables; the tray icon and its menu |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/keys.rs` | The low-level keyboard hook behind the captions key and the macros' shortcuts (`handle` is the pure part: `Outcome { swallow, events, mask }`; a macro's shortcut is the modifiers held exactly plus its key, swallowed down and up and posted as `KeyEvent::Macro { generation, index }`, the panel handing the shortcuts over by `set_macro_hotkeys` and ignoring a stale generation; every key Kiku sends carries `OWN_KEYS` in `dwExtraInfo` and only those are ignored, so another program's keys count as typed; `press` lets held modifiers go with `LIFT` and presses them again with `RESTORE`, which the hook swallows for a key the user let go meanwhile, and Windows' key state, which reads a lifted key as up, never drops a lifted key from the hook's; `KeyEvent::LetGo` once the captions key's modifiers are all up after it was held, and `holding_captions_key` until then), on a thread of its own so a busy panel never delays typing (also captures keys while a shortcut is being set, for at most 10 s), and putting text into the focused app: dictation and the paste shortcut both paste with Shift+Insert through a lent clipboard (`clipboard.rs`), since typing text floods apps and every keyboard hook with keystrokes; dictation goes through the word rules of the app it lands in (`focused_app`, named as captions name it), each batch of finished lines planned by `WordBook::plan` into steps; `press_keys` sends a step's shortcut by `inputs` (the `KeyStroke`s as `INPUT`s) through `press`, which lets go of held modifiers. Undo and growing a paste take a paste back with one Ctrl+Z (Backspaces in consoles and terminals, found by window class) |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/folds.rs` | Which sections the panel folds to one row (icon, name, Live, latest line) or hides: folded or opened by hand (which sticks), auto-folded once an app is quiet for the set time and opened when it speaks (never you), all but one for solo (undone by soloing it again); the eye-off button hides an app from the panel only (still captioned, saved, and seen by the MCP) until the "N hidden" chip. A folded section's space goes to the open ones |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/selection.rs` | Text selected by dragging across the open sections' captions, in the order shown; offsets are in the text as copied ("Name: text"), which `labels.rs` maps to the text as laid out |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/labels.rs` | How a speaker's name shows on a line in each `LabelStyle`: a `Shape` (the style and whether the line starts its turn, a turn being consecutive lines with one label), its `Lead` (the name, tag or heading put before the text in the layout), the room left of the text for a rail or badge, whether a rail or badge is drawn, the offsets between the copied and the laid out text, the sample call the settings' previews draw. One place for the rules; `render/voices.rs` draws them for the panel's rows, the previews and (through `Lead`) captions mode |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/render/voices.rs` | Names in the panel: the marks a style draws beside a line (a pill behind a tag, a rail, a round badge, a hover plate; each a click target that opens the naming box), the colour-only style's name chip under the pointer, the naming box over a line, the Apps tab's "Names on lines" (a choice and a three-line preview for the speakers' and for yours) and "Saved voices" (name in its colour, Rename in a text box, Everywhere or Only in the app it was named in, a two-press Forget; one plain line when empty) |
