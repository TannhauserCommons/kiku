# Words, dictation and macros

Word rules, voice actions, dictation, macros and their editors. Code and tests outrank this page.

| Path | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `crates/core/src/words.rs` | Word rules in `%APPDATA%\kiku\words.txt`, a plain file people and assistants edit: `from => to` rules (whole words or a phrase, any case, punctuation beside them allowed, longest first; nothing after `=>` deletes, with a comma and space after it), `@ word` to listen for, optionally with a strength of its own (`@ Lull :2.5`, above 0 up to 10; 0.2.6's `:stronger` and `:weaker` read as 2.5 and 1) (`hotwords()`, for decoding: in every app whatever its section, as the engine has one list; a word's boost is its own strength or the settings'; `WordBook::hotwords` gives none while `words.hotwords` is off), `! transform` (lowercase, no-trailing-punctuation, capitalise; in order), and `> phrase => press keys` and `> phrase => paste text` voice actions (`actions.rs`; dictation only), and `[App]` sections ("You" is the mic). Everywhere's entries run before an app's, rules before transforms; a bad line is skipped and logged. Settings say whether rules and transforms apply to captions, dictation or both (default: rules both, transforms dictation). `WordBook` (in `Controls`) rereads the file when its modified time changes, looked at most once a second on the decoder's poll, and edits it keeping comments and order (`add_rule`, `add_word`, `set_strength`, `remove`), written with `transcripts::replace`. `ASSISTANT_INSTRUCTIONS` is what the Words tab copies for an assistant; `skills/kiku/SKILL.md` describes the same |
| `crates/core/src/actions.rs`, `crates/core/src/actions/matching.rs` | Voice actions, the `>` lines of `words.txt`: `> phrase => press ctrl+shift+t` (a command: only when the whole dictated line is the phrase, punctuation and case aside; key names incl. Enter, Tab, Esc, arrows, Home/End, Page Up/Down, F1 to F24; Win+L refused) and `> phrase => paste text` (a snippet: anywhere in a dictated sentence, longest phrase first, whole words; `\n`, `\t` and `\\` escapes, the rest verbatim, ` # ` and `=>` included; said alone it is pasted exactly). Number words zero to twenty match digits. `Words::plan` turns dictated lines into `Step`s (pastes and key presses), an app's section winning over everywhere's; actions act in dictation only, never in captions, History or the MCP. A snippet's text is a `Private` (its `Debug` shows a length) and appears in no caption, History, log or MCP. `matching.rs` indexes each section when the file is read: commands by whole phrase, snippets in a trie of tokens |
| `crates/core/src/macros/` (`mod`, `file`, `compile`, `plan`, `template`, `transcript`, `triggers`, `modes`, `open`, `commands`, `palette`, `report`) | Voice macros, the core of them. `mod.rs`: `Macros` (the index over `macros.toml` and `words.txt`'s `>` lines as one-step macros; `add`/`replace`/`remove` edit its copy of the text, `parse` skips what can't load with `macros.toml:<line>` and a reason that never holds a value). `file.rs`: `macros.toml` and `variables.toml` as data (`Macro`, `StepSpec`: paste, paste_file with raw, attach, press, copy, type, wait, notify, mode, open, switch, run, ask_claude, kiku (`kiku = "open settings"` with `tab`, `open history` with `search`; `STEP_OPTIONS`); `[[mode]]` tables and a macro's `modes`; `Variables`, all `Private`), edited with `toml_edit` keeping comments, and `read_text_file` (1 MiB, UTF-8 or UTF-16, binary refused with "use attach"; `MAX_ATTACHED` 20). `compile.rs`: checks a macro and turns it into phrases (words, `{words}` (the rest of the line) and `{n:number}` slots) and steps (press sequences and `×N`/`×{slot}` repeats, at most 100). `plan.rs`: `Macros::plan` binds slots and `{heard}`, takes a dictated line's stop phrase off first (`dictation::stop_phrase`, `set_stop_phrases`) and plans an at-once `stop dictation` after it, `Step::Kiku` (`KikuStep`) handed to `Executor::kiku` as a `KikuRequest` (a templated name or tab is checked when it runs, a notice listing the valid ones), `run` drives an `Executor` and turns a step that can't run into a notice naming the macro, `preview` for the Try boxes. `template.rs`: the variable language (`{name:args|filter}`, `Context`, `Template`; `{dictating}`, `{captions}` and `{mode}` come from `Context::state` (`KikuState`) and win over `variables.toml`; transforms are `words::Transform`). `transcript.rs`: `{transcript:WHO:HOW_MUCH}` over the captions (`paragraph` is a 3 s pause between lines' first-heard times). Triggers: a macro needs `say`, `hotkey` (`keybinds::Hotkey`: modifiers in the order Ctrl, Alt, Shift, Win and one key; Ctrl, Alt or Win or F13 to F24; no Win+L or Ctrl+Alt+Delete; one macro per shortcut, the later skipped; none with a slot in its steps) or both, and `listen = true` (needs `say`) lets its phrases run as a mic's whole line with no key held. `triggers.rs`: `Macros::hotkeys(&Keybinds)` (the shortcuts to hand the hook, and those that clash with the captions key or it plus a command key, by `Keybinds::clash`), `plan_macro` (a shortcut runs it alone, `{heard}` empty), `plan_heard(line, app, wake)` over a second index of the listening macros (the wake word stripped by tokens) and `listens()`; `macros.wake` in settings. Phase 2: every plan takes the mode (`plan(lines, app, settings, &Mode)`, `plan_macro`, `plan_heard`). `modes.rs`: `Mode` (`Normal`, `Commands`, `Off`, `Own`), `Modes` (the `[[mode]]` tables: `apps` switch to it while in front, `effective(app, chosen)` gives an app's mode only while `normal` is chosen), `works_in` (a macro's `modes`; empty is every mode but `off`), and `BuiltinPhrases` ("command mode", "normal mode", "dictation off", "dictation on", "what can I say"; whole dictated lines heard before macros in every mode, each switchable, not heard by `plan_heard`); one index per mode, nothing per line beyond a length check. `open.rs`: `open_target` (http, https and mailto links, an existing file or folder on a drive, or an app name of words only; refuses other links, programs, scripts and shortcuts (`PROGRAM_EXTENSIONS`, also trailing dots and streams), network and `\\?\` paths, and quotes no value). `commands.rs`: `Command` (program inside `scripts\` or absolute, arguments as templates, `RunOutput` none/paste/notify/`variable:name`, `timeout` 1 to 60 000 ms, default 10 000), its `fingerprint` (program, arguments as written, output), `CommandPolicy` and `Approvals` (`approved-commands.txt` beside `custom\`: a fingerprint and the program file's sha256 a line). `Macros::steps` turns a `run` into a notice unless the policy, folder, approval and file contents allow it, in one place for dictation, shortcuts and listening; `run_step` fills each argument (one argument whatever a slot holds), calls the `Executor` and hands back a `variable:` output for the steps still queued. `palette.rs`: `Macros::available(app, mode)`. `report.rs`: `Customization` (the MCP tools' logic: `list_macros`, `problems`, `try_phrase`) |
| `crates/core/src/dictation.rs` | Dictation as the user controls it: `DictationSettings`, and `Dictation`, the one state machine that the key, `kiku` steps and stop phrases drive (times come from the caller): `key_down`/`key_up`/`chord`, `start`/`stop`/`toggle`/`stop_now`, `tick`, `next_wake`. Hold starts after `HOLD_AFTER` (250 ms), so a chord never dictates; toggle starts on a bare press's key-up; dictation a step or toggle starts is latched until a bare press, a stop, a stop phrase or captioning stopping (a chord leaves it on, except the one that stops captioning); the release delay keeps it on after a stop or let-go, and a press inside it carries on. `stop_phrase(line, phrases)` finds the longest phrase a line ends with (tokens, whole words) and gives the text before it |
| `crates/core/src/macros/kiku.rs` | The `kiku` step: `KikuAction::ALL` (21 actions, no quit) and `SettingsTab::ALL`, each with a name (file) and label (editor's picker) from one table, listed in errors by `Listed`; `Named<T>` (known, or a template checked when the step runs); `KikuStep { action, tab, search, at_once, from }` and `KikuRequest` for the executor. The file reader, editor, command keys (`keybinds::Command::action`), MCP tools and `macros/readme.md` (a test checks it names every action) all use it |
| `crates/core/src/file_watch.rs`, `crates/core/src/macros/book.rs` | `FileWatch`: a file's modified time, looked at most once a second (`WordBook` and `MacroBook` both use it). `MacroBook` (`Controls::macros`) holds the parsed `Macros` and `Variables` from `paths::custom_dir()` (`%APPDATA%\kiku\custom`: `macros.toml`, `variables.toml`), read again when either file, `approved-commands.txt` or `words.txt`'s `Words` changes or `words.txt`'s `Words` is a new one (`refresh(&words)`, called when dictation is planned); skipped macros are logged by place and reason; it gives its macros Settings' `BuiltinPhrases` and `CommandPolicy` (`set_phrases`, `set_policy`) and the approvals of `approved-commands.txt` (watched too; only lines that record the program's contents are trusted); the first read also writes `custom\README.md` (`macros::README`, from `macros/readme.md`: files, steps, variables, limits, privacy) when there is none, never overwriting one |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/macro_run.rs` | Running macro steps in the app in front: `MacroRun` is the `Context` (variables, clock, clipboard, selection by a lent Ctrl+C, app and window title, paths against `custom\`, the captions' transcript built once per run, word rules, random) and the `Executor` (text by one `clipboard::lend` and Shift+Insert; files by `clipboard::lend_files` (`CF_HDROP`) and Ctrl+V; `keys::press_chords`; `keys::type_text` only for a `type` step; `wait` delays the panel's next tick instead of sleeping; `switch` and `open` first send Kiku's own no-op key (`keys::tap_mask_key`) so Windows' foreground lock lets them take the front; `await_copy` waits for a copy of the selection, for `selection()` and `copy(None)`). Phase 2: `open` by `ShellExecuteW` (an app's name is looked up only among Start menu shortcuts and App Paths, never given to the shell), `switch_to` the topmost window of the app (named by `keys::app_of`), `run_command` = `run_program` (`launch`: `.exe`, `.bat`/`.cmd` by Rust's `Command`, `.ps1`, `.py`; hidden, in a kill-on-close job, 1 MiB of output, ended at its timeout), `ask_claude` through `share::send`, `set_mode` into `Controls::mode`. `Window::run_next_step` runs one planned `macros::Step` per `STEP_TIMER` tick through `macros::run_step` (150 ms, longer after a `wait`; a `run` step's variable is bound into the steps still queued; `undo_after` keeps a paste across wait, notice and mode steps); Ctrl+Win+Z and a new dictation clear the queue |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/dispatch.rs` | `Dispatch`: the panel's one way to dictation and Kiku's actions. Holds the one `Dictation`; a captions-key `KeyEvent` and every `KikuRequest` from steps come here (`key`, `perform`, `tick`, `next_wake`), the dictation actions are done here (no start while captioning is stopped; stopping captions stops dictation), the rest go back for the panel's `perform_kiku`; keeps `Controls::dictating` equal to the machine. Apart from the window, so tests drive it |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/dictation_card.rs`, `crates/app/src/overlay/render/dictation.rs` | The Dictation card in Settings > General (after Mode): key (Hold / Press), release delay (Off, 250 to 1000 ms) and the stop phrases box (a `Field`, saved on each key). `DictationCard` holds the edited settings; the window sets `Window::dictation`, `Dispatch::set`, `MacroBook::set_stop_phrases` and saves. `draw_dictation_key_row` is also setup's Keys step |
| `crates/app/src/overlay/macro_tab.rs`, `crates/app/src/overlay/render/macros.rs` | The settings window's Macros tab. `MacroTab` (`View::macros`, opened on `custom\macros.toml` when the tab is shown) lists the file's macros (a search by name or phrase, On/Off, Edit, Delete) and edits one (name, phrases with a blank always ending them, the steps with their text boxes, Record for a press step, the Add step menu offering every `StepKind` (a Kiku action step is a picker of `KikuAction::ALL` and, for open settings or history, a box for `tab` or `search`), Save, Back). Changes go through `Macros::add`/`replace`/`remove` on a copy (a refused macro keeps its reason, shown under the buttons), then `transcripts::replace` writes `macros.toml` whole, comments kept; an edit or On/Off keeps the macro's place (`Macros::replace`). Apps are edited in a Where box (apart by commas, empty is everywhere); `anywhere` and `enabled` stay as the file has them. Delete takes two presses (`armed`). The list also has the Programs card (switches `ToggleRunPrograms`/`ToggleRunAnywhere`, saved as `run_programs`; a row with Approve for each `Macros::commands()` entry whose approval is missing or recorded for other program contents (`unapproved`; Approve writes `approved-commands.txt` with `add_with_contents`, a program that isn't there approved as empty)), switches for the five built-in phrases (`mode_phrases`, given to the `MacroBook`), and ✨ Ask Claude (`Action::AskForMacro`: `custom\README.md`'s text through `share`). The editor and Add step menu (two columns) offer every `StepKind`; a run step is its program in the step's box, an Output button, and each argument a box of its own (`MacroBox::Argument`, a blank one last). Typing goes to the focused box through `field.rs` (`type_unit`, `key`); Record uses the rebinding capture (`Rebind::Macro`: keys held together, taken once let go). A step's text shows only in the editor, never in the list. `render/macros.rs` draws it, every control a `Target::Click(Action::Macro(..))` (the interaction tests in `macros_ui_tests.rs` click only what was drawn), and its tooltips. The editor's Trigger rows: Shortcut (the keys recorded by the same capture as a press step, `Recording::Hotkey`, or None; Record, Clear), a Listen switch ("Say it without the key") and the wake word box, which is a setting (`MacroBox::Wake`, saved as `macros.wake` as it is edited); `set_settings(keybinds, dictation_only, wake)` gives the tab Kiku's keys (Save refuses a shortcut that clashes with them, showing why) and the dictation only note (`listen_note`) |
